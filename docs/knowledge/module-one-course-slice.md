# Module 1 Course Slice: Authoring Plan

## Authorization and deliverable

**8 November 2026 — User-authorized:** “OK, go for it” selects the proposed Module 1 authoring pass: a short-session plan for the four grammar topics, a bounded vocabulary pack connected to vocabulary units 1–5, worked exercises/grading examples, and an engine gap list with implementation order. This is the planning subset of BL-045/BL-046. It does not select runtime implementation of the entire module or all proposed exercise families.

The packet comprises this plan, the [language pack](module-one-language-pack.md), and [worked exercises](module-one-worked-exercises.md). It implements the user's confirmed hierarchy of topic units containing multiple short sessions (Q26). Session labels below are local draft references, not new global grammar/content IDs or published practice-plan revisions. The sequence and session size are assistant authoring defaults for review.

**Outcome:** using supplied fictional identity/reference contexts, read a short present-tense personal/routine text and construct individual statements, explicit-fronting statements, neutral questions and bounded negations. Record evidence separately for meaning, the named grammar/lexical target, task constraints and linguistic certainty. This is not an A1 completion or learning-efficacy claim; language remains a source-checked draft awaiting Danish teaching review.

## Scope and coverage

| Parent topic from the user's blueprint | Included in this slice | Explicit limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Pronouns and `være` | Seven human subject pronouns; stored `er`; identifying `Det er …`; supplied identity/profession complements; singular referential `den/det` with article/gender given. | No adjective paradigm, object/plural reference, general demonstratives or dummy-subject system. Supplied `Jeg er lærer` is one reviewed frame, not a universal article rule. |
| 2. Present/infinitive and frequent irregulars | Stored regular present forms; possession `har`; naming/residence; ability `kan` plus bare infinitive; selected meanings of `vil/skal/må` in explicit contexts. | No person agreement, tense choice, past/future system, epistemic modals, prohibition/obligation negation contrasts or general `at` complements. |
| 3. Main clauses and V2 | Subject-first order; one final circumstance; time or place fronting as one constituent. | V2 means second constituent, not second word. No object-fronting curriculum, multiple fronted units, marked order or subordinate clauses. |
| 4. Questions and negation | Neutral affirmative yes/no questions; `hvad/hvor/hvornår/hvorfor/hvordan` recognition; constrained object/place/time wh production; separate subject `hvem`; `ikke`, fronted-clause negation, `kan ikke`, and `aldrig`. | `hvordan` initially occurs in the supplied chunk `Hvordan har du det?`; no reason-clause answers, embedded questions, wh role ambiguity, negative questions, `ja/jo` polarity system or unrestricted negation scope. |

Vocabulary units 1–5 provide **contexts**, not five completed word lists. Greetings and fictional identity are scaffolding; routines supply reading/sleeping/work; time starts with short chunks; family contexts establish explicitly stated person/group references. Countries/nationalities, numbers 0–100, clock/calendar systems, a 25-verb course inventory and the broader family lexicon remain later authoring work. Full Level 1 vocabulary coverage must not be inferred from this slice.

## Ordered short sessions

`P1–P4` identify the four parent topics; `U1–U5` identify vocabulary themes. Every assessed item has one primary target. Other known forms and meanings are supplied or reviewed. Count first learner exposure even when an entry already exists in the software seed; the learner is a complete beginner.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S01 / P1 | Singular human subject reference, G003 subset | Introduce `jeg/du/han/hun` in small rounds, supplied `hej`, names and `er`; reference/pronouns stated explicitly, never inferred from appearance. | Select the subject for “I am Anna”: `Jeg er Anna.` Only pronoun choice is assessed. |
| S02 / P1 | Group subject reference, G003 subset | Introduce `vi/I/de`; supply `sove/sover` and English family/group descriptions; review singular subjects. | `Vi sover.` Distinguish a speaking group, several addressees and a mentioned group. |
| S03 / P1 | Supply invariant `er`, G004 subset | Introduce `at være → er`, fixed identifying L008 `det` in `Det er …`, `en bog`, `et brev`, `en lærer`; review human subjects. Occupation article omission is explained, not assessed. |
| S04 / P1 | Singular referential `den/det`, local G038 subset | Article/gender and explicit inanimate antecedent supplied; teach both reference uses separately from fixed identifying `det`. | Given `et brev`, select `det` for referential “it”. No object pronouns or unintroduced noun inflection. |
| S05 / P2 | Named verb's regular present, G004 | Introduce `arbejde/arbejder`, `læse/læser`, `en avis`; review `sove/sover`. Supply subject and noun phrases. | `Hun læser en avis.` Distinguish `læse/læser`, not unknown verb meanings. |
| S06 / P3 | Subject-first assembly, G005 | Review introduced finite verbs/noun bundles; optional reused `spise/spiser + et æble` is taught before inclusion, otherwise omitted. | `Jeg læser en bog.` Initial blocks keep the noun phrase together; word tiles are a later scaffold. |
| S07 / P2 | Retrieve stored `har`, G004 subset | Introduce possession `have/har`; review known subjects and reading-material nouns. | `Han har et brev.` `har` is not an auxiliary here. |
| S08 / P2 vocabulary bridge | Introduced reading-material meaning, lexical target | Introduce only `et magasin` through a reading situation; review `en bog/en avis/et brev`, introduced separately in S03/S05/S07. | Four related English→Danish choices; assess the named sense, not gender or article recall. |
| S09 / P2 | Supply a named verb's present, G004 subset | Introduce `hedde/hedder`, `bo/bor`, `tale/taler + dansk` in separate short rounds; supply `i + Aarhus/Odense` and fictional names. | `Jeg hedder Anna.` / `Jeg bor i Aarhus.` / `Jeg taler dansk.` The named lemma and intended meaning are supplied in form assessments. |
| S10 / P2 | Bare infinitive after supplied `kan`, G030/C014 | Introduce ability `kunne/kan`; review `læse` and the already introduced language complement `dansk`. | `Jeg kan læse et brev.` / `Jeg kan læse dansk.` Contrast bare infinitive, finite present and infinitive with `at`; the language-complement reading frame is explicitly supplied. |
| S11 / P2 | Interpret selected modal meanings, G030 subset | Introduce `ville/vil`, `skulle/skal`, `måtte/må` in separate rounds with explicit willingness/intention, obligation and permission contexts. | Interpret a supplied sentence, then construct it with its supplied modal. No ambiguous English “will/must/can” choice task. |
| S12 / P3 | Add one final circumstance, G016 | Introduce `i dag` and `nu`; supplied place chunk `i Aarhus` and optional `hjemme`; meanings given. | `Jeg arbejder i dag.` Clause core is supplied. Do not assess time vocabulary and order in the same item. |
| S13 / P4 | Neutral yes/no inversion, G018/C010 | Review clauses; supply `ja/nej` as glossed response support. | `Læser du en avis?` Construct from an explicit proposition; no English-style `do`. |
| S14 / P3 | Front one constituent with V2, G021/C008 | Review S12 circumstances and S13 inversion; specify exactly which constituent must be first. | `I dag arbejder jeg.` / `I Aarhus bor jeg.` Keep the two-word first field intact in the initial scaffold. |
| S15 / P4 | Simple main-clause `ikke`, G017/C009 | Introduce `ikke`; supply a neutral subject-first intransitive clause. | `Jeg arbejder ikke.` No ambiguous object-negation contrast. |
| S16 / P4 | `ikke` after the postverbal subject | Review fronting and `ikke`; supply the correct V2 skeleton. | `I dag arbejder jeg ikke.` Only negation position is assessed. |
| S17 / P4 | `ikke` before a modal's infinitive | Review ability `kan`; context explicitly means inability. | `Jeg kan ikke arbejde.` Exclude `må ikke/skal ikke` scope work. |
| S18 / P4 | Interpret/place `aldrig` in a neutral main clause | Introduce `aldrig`, with supplied `hjemme`; review `arbejde`. | `Jeg arbejder aldrig hjemme.` Keep `aldrig` and `ikke` as alternatives, not a beginner double negative. |
| S19 / P4 | Select non-subject question expression, G019 subset | Introduce `hvad/hvor/hvornår/hvorfor/hvordan` over separate contextual rounds; supply the rest of each question and any reason/manner information. | `Hvornår arbejder du?` Recognize `Hvordan har du det?` as a complete supported chunk, not productive grammar. |
| S20 / P4 | Order a non-subject wh question, G019/C011 | Intended question word and requested information supplied; review naming, residence and reading. | `Hvad læser hun?` / `Hvor bor du?` / `Hvornår arbejder du?` No duplicate object or stranded preposition. |
| S21 / P4 | Subject `hvem` without a second subject | Introduce `hvem` with explicit requested role; review `sove/arbejde`. | `Hvem arbejder i dag?` `hvem` fills the subject position. No role-ambiguous “who sees Anna?” item. |

Each session card contains a short English explanation, an annotated correct example, a supported item, a contrast, a changed-combination check and a constrained constructed response. This is an authoring template, not a fixed duration or verified optimal dose. Provide optional gloss/reference support and record reveals. Answer-bearing examples must differ from the assessment proposition; hiding a matching solution in a collapsed lesson still constitutes possible answer exposure.

Use the [language ledger](module-one-language-pack.md) to introduce every required form before assessing it. A supplied noun phrase can support a grammar exercise without becoming a gender assessment. Initial word-choice surfaces use consistent article+noun bundles as an authoring default; Q27 concerns **typed recall**, which is not changed here.

Preserve original C001–C003 published variants. The candidate module uses explicitly licensed variants of C004 (identity), C006 (residence with `i + place`), C007 (circumstances), C008 (fronting), C009 (negation), C010/C011/C012 (questions) and C014 (modal plus infinitive). A family reference is not permission to mix every lexical filler or optional slot. Every authored proposition records selected sense/frame, subject, complement, circumstance, polarity and speech act.

Local variants requiring reconciliation before runtime activation:

- G022's current adjective prerequisites do not define this narrow supplied identity/profession case; do not mark all of G022 completed.

- G004 excludes modals; S10/S11 use G030/C014. The modal's introduced meaning and the following bare infinitive are separate assessment facets.
- G021 currently needs G016/G018: S12/S13 precede S14 even though the parent topics are displayed in another order. Session navigation must not equate parent-topic numbering with every prerequisite edge.
- G017's original simple-clause subset does not itself activate fronted/modal negation or `aldrig`; S16–S18 have named composite variants.
- G019 defers subject wh questions; S21 needs an explicit local target tied to C012 before activation. Do not assign a new stable G identifier until its scope is reviewed.

## Module outcome and completion evidence

An original final review text can be: `Jeg hedder Anna. Jeg bor i Aarhus. I dag læser jeg en avis. Jeg kan læse dansk.` Use fictional profiles, not a requirement to disclose personal details.

Ask which place or reading item is **explicitly stated**, rather than treating absent facts as false. Collect separate one-sentence responses to bounded cues about name, residence and today's action, followed by a specified question or negation. Each item retains its own target; do not collapse the resulting message into an unsupported free-writing score. Reuse introduced meanings in changed, licensed combinations. Shuffling an identical sentence or immediately correcting it is not an unseen transfer case.

An unseen combination means a new licensed proposition/order task built from introduced language, not an untaught word. Track novelty separately for lexical sense, proposition and presentation. S08's first drill immediately after the magazine introduction is supported practice; a later closed-reference retrieval can assess recall while still recording earlier teaching and any exact-answer exposure.

Working completion rule: the planned activities have been attempted and feedback reviewed, including the constructed response. A confirmed wrong answer may remain in the evidence; a timeout/unevaluable result is not a learner error or a completed assessment. Keep supported retries, first attempts, known versus unknown language diagnosis and prior answer exposure distinct. Learner-controlled repeat/review is allowed. No numerical mastery threshold, automatic scheduler, all-skills level or correctness-based course lock is selected.

Q27 (typed noun-only versus bundled recall) and Q28 (vocabulary budget granularity) remain open. This packet uses article-bearing teaching, supplies noun phrases in grammar tasks, and tracks new/reused items without treating 15–20 as a per-session quota. Neither open choice prevents reviewing the worked module.
