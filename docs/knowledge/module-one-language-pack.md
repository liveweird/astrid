# Module 1 Language Pack

## Status and counting

**8 November 2026 — Source-checked authoring draft** for the [authorized course slice](module-one-course-slice.md). These are candidate forms, senses and frame permissions, not additions to the running vocabulary database. Independent source review supports the bounded examples; Danish teaching approval, learner validation and corpus-frequency ranking remain separate.

The core reuses **17 existing seed identities**, proposes **23 new authoring packets**, and names **six supplied glue packets**. Packets group teaching material: they are not a count of unique words or normalized database records. In particular, a multiword packet can reference several lexical components or new sense/frame analyses. Names/placeholders and optional seed extensions are disclosed separately. This is a small Module 1 subset of vocabulary themes 1–5, not their complete 75–100-item inventory or the proposed “top 25 verbs.”

When publishing, assign stable lexical/sense IDs only after checking the catalogue. Preserve existing lemma identity where appropriate, but do not reuse a selected-sense ID for a new meaning or mutate frozen construction permissions. Every component needed by a word-tile response must have explicit lexical identity, even if taught as a chunk.

## Exact frame permissions and exclusions

- **Identity:** fixed identifying `Det er + supplied NP` remains distinct from `S er NAME` and the one supplied occupation `S er lærer`. Do not infer a universal “professions never take articles” rule. The same noun is introduced as `en lærer`; supported identifying `Det er en lærer` belongs to a different frame.
- **Residence:** `S bor i PLACE`, with one supplied place chunk. Its fronted version is `I PLACE bor S`. The preposition retains its lexical lowercase identity; sentence-initial capitalization is display behavior.
- **Reading:** `S læser NP` for the four selected written-material senses; explicitly permit one `i dag` or `nu` circumstance, final (`S læser NP i dag`) or fronted with V2 (`I dag læser S NP`, `Nu læser S NP`). This licenses the final review's `I dag læser jeg en avis`. `S kan læse dansk` uses a separately licensed language complement, not arbitrary substitution of any noun for an object. Do not license reading persons, furniture or vehicles from word class alone.
- **Work/sleep:** intransitive human subject with at most one introduced circumstance in initial sessions; `arbejde` permits `i dag`, `nu`, `hjemme` or `i + PLACE` in the authored subset. The expressly named composites are `S arbejder aldrig hjemme` and, for the duplicate-token acceptance fixture only, `S arbejder i PLACE i dag`. Teach the latter complete variant before any learner assessment; it does not license arbitrary multiple-adverbial ordering or expand the initial one-circumstance sessions. Sleep keeps the simpler subset.
- **Modal:** selected `kan/vil/skal/må` + an introduced bare-infinitive frame. Negation initially extends only ability `kan`. Other scope combinations are recorded as deferred, not invalid Danish.
- **Reference:** early `den/det` production is subject-only. A source example such as `Nu læser jeg den` uses an object pronoun and is not licensed by the Module 1 subject-reference subset.
- **Wh:** identify the requested missing role before realizing the question. `hvem` as subject replaces the subject; a non-subject wh question retains one explicit subject. Avoid `Hvem ser Anna?` role ambiguity, preposition stranding and answer production requiring `fordi`.

New frame permissions must be associated with the future content snapshot. Do not broaden the old C001–C003 or reuse their stored fingerprints. The [course plan](module-one-course-slice.md#sentence-and-vocabulary-licensing) names draft family links and prerequisites; it does not activate all variants of those families.

## Fairness and source limits

The four-choice pool is `bog/avis/brev/magasin` in its introduced senses. `blad` was considered and rejected as an active alternative here: DDO sense 2 also covers a newspaper, creating an actual ambiguity beside `avis`. This is not evidence that `blad` is an invalid translation of magazine/newspaper. [DDO blad, sense 2](https://ordnet.dk/ddo/ordbog/blad).

Present-form and modal explanations are supported by [DSN verbs](https://sproget.dk/typiske-problemer/verber-udsagnord/) and [Peter Harder's infinitive account](https://lex.dk/infinitiv), which explicitly describes bare infinitives after modals. For V2 versus neutral finite-verb-first questions, see [DSN's word-order discussion](https://dsn.dk/nyt-fra-sprognaevnet/maj-2021/fire-maader-hvorpaa-engelsk-har-praeget-dansk-grammatik/). Subject `hvem` has the dedicated DSN source linked above.

Only compact forms, selected grammatical facts and original teaching examples are recorded. Dictionary definitions/corpus examples are not imported as a content dataset; bulk reuse remains BL-025. Every packet still needs its final authoring provenance, accepted-alternative checks and teaching-review status before activation. Sources support language facts, not the optimal session sequence, a frequency claim, mastery or learning efficacy.
