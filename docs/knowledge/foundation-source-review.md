## Resolved review disagreements

G028 already explicitly defers auxiliary `være`; the proposed missing-deferral finding was rejected. The lead also rejected a claim that the marked-conditional source lacks written-corpus evidence: its printed p. 373 supplies supplementary observations. These checks prevent narrower source inspection from becoming an incorrect correction.

No contradiction was confirmed in C017's broad understood-subject wording. Control, raising and omitted infinitive markers still need dedicated later treatment. A no-finding disposition is bounded by the reviewed examples and sources; it is not an exhaustive endorsement of all future combinations.

## Coverage and remaining work

The later map still needs reciprocal G↔C authoring links and variant-specific support requirements. The structural checker verifies existing IDs/references and the prerequisite graph, not pedagogical sufficiency or semantic licensing.

- **Identified, deferred:** embedded subject questions, finite content clauses functioning as subjects, unstressed object-pronoun placement with negation, dedicated particle frames, comparison with `end`, broader participle/passive distinctions and marked register variants.
- **Source-limited:** G053's wider tense-perspective account needs a stronger dedicated reference. English equivalents, naturalness, discourse context and teaching order need appropriate Danish teaching review.
- **Not a complete formal grammar:** “all variants” remains an unresolved coverage boundary. The catalogue names further extraction, ellipsis, presentation, cleft and register families without promising engine support.

After these corrections the graph contains 58 unique skills and 123 prerequisite edges, 30 constructions, 23 lexical records and 16 grammar sources. A final independent lead re-review found no additional actionable issue in the grammar/catalogue diff and confirmed the active pilot records were unchanged. The offline structural guard passed; the inventory count is descriptive, not a permanent size limit.
