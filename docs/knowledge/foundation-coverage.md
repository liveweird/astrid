# Foundation Skill-to-Construction Coverage

## Status and use

This is an **assistant-proposed authoring crosswalk** between the stable `G001`–`G058` skills in the [grammar map](danish-grammar-map.md) and the stable `C001`–`C030` families in the [sentence catalogue](danish-sentence-catalogue.md). It closes a documentation linkage gap; it does not add IDs, linguistic rules, vocabulary, exercise formats, runtime support, or a teaching-sequence decision. The draft remains subject to Danish-language teaching review and makes no CEFR claim.

Only C001–C003 are implemented, within their positive-present pilot restrictions. Their links distinguish assessed targets from supporting knowledge:

- **PT — pilot target:** directly assessed by an existing pilot format: G004, G005, or G006.
- **PS — pilot support:** required by pilot content, but not independently assessed by an existing format: G001, G002, or G003.
- **O — later overlay:** a bounded form, phrase, or meaning overlay on a described construction family. It is unsupported and does not activate that family.
- **X — crosscut:** analysis, spelling, or punctuation applicable only after a construction is otherwise licensed.
- **D — gap/deferred:** the current catalogue does not describe the target sufficiently; explicit new or broadened reviewed coverage is needed.

A C reference is therefore a family pointer, not permission to generate all its variants. Every later form still needs reviewed lexical frames, meanings, prerequisites, context, and construction-specific restrictions.

## Human review fields and unresolved coverage

Future authoring review should record: `G ID`, `C family`, link status, exact included variant, excluded variants, additional skill/lexical/context prerequisites, linguistic-review status, and engine-support status. Keeping “included” and “excluded” explicit prevents a C029 overlay or generic clause family from being mistaken for all-variant support.

The principal unresolved structural links are particle frames (G033), comparison syntax with `end` (part of G050), finite content-clause subjects, embedded subject questions, and marked order (G058). Additional structural gaps include subclausal coordination, object-pronoun placement with negation, and broader ellipsis. Standalone indefinite/negative pronouns require reviewed lexical/context and polarity licensing; they can fill existing general clause slots and are not inherently a new structural family. Nominalization examples and compound formation/spelling are also lexical authoring work whose reviewed noun results may use an already licensed noun slot. G053 remains source-limited. None of these gaps implies a new ID or authorizes implementation.
